### 🏗️ **Software Engineering Excellence**

#### **Architecture Patterns**
- ✅ **Component-Based Architecture** - Reusable, maintainable components
- ✅ **Separation of Concerns** - UI, logic, and data layers separated
- ✅ **Context API** - Centralized state management
- ✅ **Custom Hooks** - Encapsulated business logic
- ✅ **TypeScript** - Full type safety

#### **SOLID Principles**
- ✅ **Single Responsibility** - Each component/hook has one purpose
- ✅ **Open-Closed** - Easy to extend without modification
- ✅ **Dependency Inversion** - Components depend on abstractions

#### **Code Quality**
- ✅ **DRY** - No code duplication

## 🚀 **How to Use**

1. **Navigate** - Click sidebar items to switch views
2. **Search** - Type in the search bar to filter tracks
3. **Play Music** - Click any track card to start playing
4. **Control Playback** - Use bottom player bar
5. **Adjust Volume** - Use volume slider on the right
6. **Shuffle/Repeat** - Click buttons in the player bar
